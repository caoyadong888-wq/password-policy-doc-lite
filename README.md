# Password & Access Policy (lite)

## English

### Purpose

A free slice of the password and access policy: six sections in English and Chinese, a short checklist, and a filled Harbor & Pine excerpt for those same sections. The full pack adds the other sections (roles, account types, storage, rotation, service credentials, shared accounts, joiner mover leaver, logging, exceptions, review), the heading checker, and the 17-row checklist.

Decision tokens such as `ORG_MIN_LENGTH` are organization choices. Read NIST Special Publication 800-63B and your identity provider's docs before copying a number. The pack does not name a vendor API field. Mailboxes use example.com. Addresses are 10.8.0.0/24 and 192.0.2.10.

### Deliverables

- `deliverables/password_basics.en.md` — English template, six sections.
- `deliverables/password_basics.zh.md` — Chinese template, six sections.
- `deliverables/checklist_lite.md` — six checklist rows, status `open`.
- `examples/harbor-pine-basics.en.md` — filled English excerpt.
- `examples/harbor-pine-basics.zh.md` — filled Chinese excerpt.
- `examples/harbor-pine-basics-checklist.md` — those six rows answered for Harbor & Pine.

### Quick start

Read `examples/harbor-pine-basics.en.md` next to `deliverables/password_basics.en.md`. Copy the template, keep the `##` headings, and replace each token. Score the draft with `deliverables/checklist_lite.md`.

## 中文

### 用途

密码与访问策略的免费子集：中英各六节、一张短检查单，以及 Harbor & Pine 在同样六节上的填好节选。完整版还有其余章节（角色、账号类型、存储、轮换、服务凭证、共享账号、入职调动离职、日志、例外、复审）、标题检查程序，以及 17 行检查单。

`ORG_MIN_LENGTH` 这类记号是组织自己的决定。抄数字之前先阅读 NIST Special Publication 800-63B，并核对身份提供者的文档。本包不写厂商 API 字段。邮箱使用 example.com。地址是 10.8.0.0/24 和 192.0.2.10。

### 交付清单

- `deliverables/password_basics.en.md` — 英文模板，六节。
- `deliverables/password_basics.zh.md` — 中文模板，六节。
- `deliverables/checklist_lite.md` — 六行检查单，状态为 `open`。
- `examples/harbor-pine-basics.en.md` — 填好的英文节选。
- `examples/harbor-pine-basics.zh.md` — 填好的中文节选。
- `examples/harbor-pine-basics-checklist.md` — Harbor & Pine 作答的这六行。

### 快速开始

把 `examples/harbor-pine-basics.en.md` 和 `deliverables/password_basics.en.md` 对照着读。复制模板，保留 `##` 标题，并替换每个记号。用 `deliverables/checklist_lite.md` 给草稿打分。
