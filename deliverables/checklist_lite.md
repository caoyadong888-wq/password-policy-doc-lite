# Password basics checklist

Six rows from the full checklist. Status starts as `open`. Set it to `Yes`, `No`, or `NA` when you adopt the draft.

# 口令基础检查单

完整检查单中的六行。状态一开始是 `open`。采用草稿时写成 `Yes`、`No` 或 `NA`。

| ID | Check | 检查 | Status | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C01 | The policy names who it covers and which systems it covers. | 策略写明覆盖的人员和系统。 | open | Name the people and the systems. |
| C04 | Minimum lengths are explicit organization choices, and NIST SP 800-63B is named as reading. | 最短长度是明确的组织决定，并指出应阅读 NIST SP 800-63B。 | open | Record the lengths and where you read the publication. |
| C06 | Remote access and privileged access require more than one factor. | 远程访问和特权访问需要多于一种因素。 | open | Name the factor you enroll. |
| C08 | Privileged work uses a separate account from daily work. | 特权操作使用与日常账号分开的账号。 | open | Name the account suffix. |
| C10 | Administration from outside the office LAN uses the named bastion and MFA. | 从办公局域网以外进行管理时，走指定的堡垒机并使用多因素。 | open | Name the bastion. |
| C15 | Credential compromise has a sequence: disable, revoke, rotate, notify, review. | 凭证失陷有顺序：停用、撤销会话、轮换、通知、复核。 | open | Point at the sequence. |
